English

How to play: Swipe up, down, left, or right to slide all tiles. Tiles with the same fruit merge into the next fruit when they collide. Reach the 🍑 (2048) tile to win — then keep going for an even higher score!

Undo: Tap the Undo button to take back your last move.

Board size: Choose 3×3, 4×4, or 5×5 from the start screen for an easier or harder game.

Continue a game: If you leave mid-game, tap "Continue" on the start screen to pick up where you left off.

Having a problem, or a suggestion? Email gongaiyouxiang@gmail.com — please include your device model and iOS version if reporting a bug.

See also our Privacy Policy.

简体中文

玩法：向上下左右滑动即可移动所有方块。相同水果的方块相撞时会合并成下一种水果。 合成 🍑（2048）即获胜——之后仍可继续挑战更高分！

撤销：点击"撤销"按钮可以撤销上一步。

棋盘大小：在首页可选择 3×3、4×4 或 5×5，调整游戏难度。

继续游戏：若中途离开，回到首页点击"继续"即可接续进度。
