1. 概述Overview

Fruit 2048（以下简称"本应用"）是一款离线益智游戏。我们非常重视你的隐私，本政策说明本应用在使用过程中涉及哪些数据、如何处理，以及第三方服务（广告）可能收集的信息。

Fruit 2048 ("the App") is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains what data is involved when you use the App, how it is handled, and what information third-party services (advertising) may collect.

2. 我们收集的数据Data We Collect

本应用不要求注册或登录，不收集你的姓名、邮箱、电话等个人身份信息。

游戏进度、分数、设置等数据仅保存在你的设备本地（本地数据库与本地存储），不会上传到我们的服务器，我们也无法访问这些数据。卸载应用或清除应用数据会永久删除这些本地记录。

The App does not require an account or login, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or other personally identifiable information.

Game progress, scores, and settings are stored only locally on your device (local database and local storage). This data is never uploaded to our servers, and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data permanently deletes these local records.

3. 第三方服务：广告Third-Party Services: Advertising

本应用使用 Google AdMob（Google LLC 提供）展示广告，以支持免费提供本应用。AdMob 在展示和衡量广告效果的过程中，可能会收集广告标识符（Advertising ID）、设备信息、大致位置、IP 地址等数据，并可能使用这些数据进行广告个性化。

这些数据的收集与处理受 Google 自身的隐私政策约束，与本应用开发者无关：

  • Google 隐私权政策
  • Google 广告技术说明

The App uses Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to display ads that support offering the App for free. In the course of serving and measuring ads, AdMob may collect data such as the Advertising ID, device information, approximate location, and IP address, and may use this data for ad personalization.

Collection and processing of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policy, independent of the App's developer:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses advertising technologies

4. 权限说明Permissions

本应用请求网络相关权限（INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE），仅用于 AdMob SDK 请求和展示广告，不用于其他用途。

The App requests network-related permissions (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) solely so the AdMob SDK can request and display ads. They are not used for any other purpose.

5. 数据分享与出售Data Sharing & Sale

我们不会出售你的任何数据。除上述用于展示广告的 Google AdMob 之外，本应用不会与其他第三方共享数据。

We do not sell your data. Other than Google AdMob as described above for ad delivery, the App does not share data with any other third party.

本应用不专门面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为你的孩子在未经同意的情况下向我们提供了信息，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，我们会及时处理。

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided information to us without consent, please contact us at the email below and we will address it promptly.

你可以随时在设备的"设置 > Google > 广告"中选择退出个性化广告，或重置你的广告标识符。

You can opt out of personalized ads, or reset your Advertising ID, at any time via your device's Settings > Google > Ads.
