Last updated: August 16, 2026

English

Fruit 2048 does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The app runs entirely on your device:

  • Your best score, game progress, and settings (sound, vibration, language, board size) are saved only in your device's local storage (Apple's standard UserDefaults). They are never sent anywhere.
  • The app makes no network requests. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • The app does not require an account, login, or any personal information.
  • The app does not access your location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts.

If you uninstall the app, all locally stored data is deleted with it.

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future update adds a feature that uses data), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Questions? Contact gongaiyouxiang@gmail.com.
