Fruit 2048 for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy covers the Android version of Fruit 2048, which is supported by advertising. The iOS version contains no advertising and collects no data at all; it has its own policy.

English

The short version

We — the developer of Fruit 2048 — do not collect, store, or receive any personal information about you. Your scores and settings never leave your device. However, the Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's advertising SDK does collect certain device data in order to serve those ads. That collection is described below.

Data stored on your device only

Your best score, in-progress game, and settings (sound, vibration, language, board size) are saved in the app's private local storage on your device. This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Data collected by Google AdMob (our advertising provider)

To display ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK embedded in this app may collect and process:

Data typePurpose
Advertising ID (a resettable identifier assigned by your device)Serving and measuring ads; limiting repeated ads
Device information (model, operating system version, language, screen size)Delivering correctly formatted ads
IP address, and coarse location derived from it (typically country or region level)Serving regionally appropriate ads and complying with regional law
Ad interaction data (ads shown, tapped, or closed)Measuring ad performance and preventing fraud

This data is collected by Google, not by us — we never see it in an identifiable form. We receive only aggregate, anonymous earnings reports. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and, for advertising specifically, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device and Android version). You can reset the ID or delete it entirely, which stops it being used to personalize ads.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: in the same Android settings screen. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on your advertising ID.
  • Remove everything: uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored game data.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

No account, no other tracking

The app has no accounts, logins, or sign-ups. Beyond the Google Mobile Ads SDK described above, it contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. It does not access your location services, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or files. The only Android permission the app itself requests is VIBRATE, for haptic feedback during play; the ads SDK adds internet access and the advertising ID permission.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the "Last updated" date above. Questions or requests: gongaiyouxiang@gmail.com.
