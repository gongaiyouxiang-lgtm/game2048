Screenshots

Play the TestFlight build on your iPhone and use the side + volume-up buttons to capture three moments: the start screen, a mid-game board with a few merges visible, and a high score / big fruit moment. 3–5 screenshots is plenty.

The app is iPhone-only, so App Store Connect will only ask for one iPhone screenshot set (no iPad set). It will tell you the exact pixel size it needs for your device class — that's changed a few times over the years, so trust what the page asks for over anything written here. If your phone isn't the largest current iPhone, ASC's uploader still accepts your device's native screenshots for that size slot.

Build & App Review Information

  • Under Build, select the latest successfully processed TestFlight build (the one with the working slide/merge fix).

Submit

Click Add for Review, then Submit to App Review. Apple's review is typically 24–48 hours. You'll get an email either way — if it's rejected, the reason is almost always fixable from the notes they send.
